    Why this cadence works

    • ~135 hours total, 1.5–2 h/day, six days a week — the most-recommended CISSP cadence for working professionals.
    • Weeks 1–10 domain study · Week 11 full-exam practice + remediation · Week 12 review, rest, exam.
    • Three weekly checkpoints (Wed quiz, Sat timed block, Sun review) prevent slippage.
    • Most failed attempts share one pattern: too much reading, not enough question-bank practice. Target 2,000+ questions.

      Three ways to reach it when you are away from your desktop

      1. Cloud server (recommended). Deploy with one of the scripts in deploy/aws, deploy/azure or deploy/gcp. You get a permanent public address. Set APP_PASSWORD so only you can log in.
      2. Tunnel from your desktop (free, 1 minute). Keep the server running at home and run deploy/tunnel.sh. It opens a public https://…trycloudflare.com link that forwards to this app for as long as the tunnel runs. Set APP_PASSWORD first.
      3. Private network (most secure). Install Tailscale (or any VPN) on the desktop and your phone; then the LAN address above works from anywhere, with no port open to the internet.
      Progress is saved to the server's Excel workbook (/api/progress) and mirrored to this browser. Health: /healthz.
